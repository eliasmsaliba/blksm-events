    Why We Chose Them

    Why Wollies?

    Wollies is not a distant national organisation operating outside our immediate environment. It is part of our community.

    Located in Hesteapark in Pretoria North, Wollies has grown from a small sterilisation initiative established in 2003 into a large-scale animal shelter caring for abandoned, neglected and abused dogs and cats. It operates as a non-profit, pro-life organisation and does not receive government funding.

    Animals remain in Wollies' care until suitable forever homes can be found for them. The daily operation is supported by a dedicated team of approximately 22 employees, with volunteers providing additional support — particularly over weekends.

    Every day, the Wollies team provides food, clean water, safe shelter, bedding, veterinary treatment, medication, sanitation, exercise, socialisation and human care to animals that may have experienced abandonment, trauma, neglect or abuse.

    More Than a Shelter

    A Promise to Animals Who Have Nowhere Else to Go.

    • An abandoned dog will receive another meal.
    • A frightened cat will have somewhere warm and safe to sleep.
    • An injured animal will receive medical attention.
    • Animals will not be forgotten simply because finding them the right home may take time.

    Adoption fees include sterilisation, deworming, vaccination and microchipping. Cats are also tested for FIV and feline leukaemia before leaving the shelter. Their commitment carries a significant operational cost — veterinary expenses alone can reach approximately R200,000 per month.

    Wollies cannot carry this responsibility alone. It depends on individuals, volunteers, businesses and community organisations choosing to help. That is where BLKSM Events intends to make a difference.

    Our Initiative

    The BLKSM Monthly Wollies Drive

    One community. One collection. Hundreds of lives supported. Every month, our team travels through Pretoria and surrounding areas to collect essential supplies donated by individuals, families, businesses, schools, residential estates and corporate partners.

    01

    Priority List Announced

    Before each drive, BLKSM Events publishes a list of the items most urgently required by Wollies — so donors give according to real needs, not assumptions.

    02

    The Community Collects

    Individuals, families, schools, offices and neighbourhoods gather their contributions. A single bag of food matters. An office-wide campaign matters even more.

    03

    BLKSM Collects

    Our team travels the announced route and visits participating collection points. Larger collections from businesses and estates are arranged in advance.

    04

    Donations Sorted

    Items are inspected, grouped and prepared for delivery to ensure the handover is organised, efficient and useful to the shelter.

    05

    Delivered to Wollies

    The collected goods are transported to Wollies and formally handed over to the shelter team in Hesteapark, Pretoria North.

    06

    Impact Shared

    Following each drive, we publish an update showing what was collected, who participated and how the community contributed — including handover photos.

    The Bigger Picture

    Why Animal Welfare Belongs to All of Us

    Animal welfare is not only the responsibility of shelters and rescue organisations. It is a community responsibility.

    Shelters frequently receive animals because of abandonment, financial hardship, housing restrictions, uncontrolled breeding and owners who are no longer able or willing to care for their pets. Recent reporting from Pretoria indicates that shelters are facing growing pressure as more animals are surrendered because of financial strain and restrictive residential pet policies.

    By supporting Wollies, our community is not only helping the animals already inside the shelter. We are also contributing to a broader culture of responsible ownership, compassion and prevention.

    The BLKSM Monthly Wollies Drive will not be treated as a once-off publicity exercise. Animal welfare will become part of how we operate, communicate and engage with our community — permanently.
