1. Introduction

BLKSM Events (PTY) Ltd ("BLKSM Events", "we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting the privacy, confidentiality, and integrity of all personal information entrusted to us.

As an event management, production, marketing, and experiential services company operating in the Republic of South Africa, we process personal information in accordance with:

  • The Protection of Personal Information Act, No. 4 of 2013 (POPIA)
  • The Promotion of Access to Information Act, No. 2 of 2000 (PAIA)
  • Applicable South African electronic communications and consumer protection legislation

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, process, store, protect, and manage your personal information when you interact with our business, services, digital platforms, websites, events, suppliers, campaigns, and affiliated services.

By engaging with BLKSM Events, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

7. Legal Basis for Processing

Under POPIA, we process personal information based on:

  • Consent from the data subject
  • Contractual necessity
  • Compliance with legal obligations
  • Legitimate business interests
  • Protection of legitimate interests of the data subject
  • Performance of public law duties where applicable

8. Sharing of Information

We may share personal information with trusted third parties where necessary, including payment processors, website hosting providers, event venues, security providers, production teams, marketing platforms, logistics companies, sponsorship partners, government authorities where legally required, and professional advisors including accountants and attorneys.

All third parties are required to maintain confidentiality and appropriate security safeguards. We do not sell personal information.

9. Direct Marketing

BLKSM Events may send promotional communications regarding upcoming events, brand campaigns, entertainment experiences, promotional offers, ticket sales, and sponsorship opportunities.

You may opt out of marketing communications at any time by clicking unsubscribe links, emailing hello@blksm.events, or requesting removal via direct communication. We comply with POPIA direct marketing requirements.

10. Cookies and Website Tracking

Our website may use cookies and related technologies to improve website performance, analyze visitor behavior, remember user preferences, improve user experience, and conduct advertising and remarketing campaigns.

Users may disable cookies through browser settings. Some website functionality may be affected.

11. Storage and Retention of Information

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill contractual obligations, complete event management processes, maintain financial records, meet legal and tax obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce contractual rights.

Once retention is no longer required, information is securely deleted or anonymized.

12. Security Safeguards

BLKSM Events implements reasonable technical and organizational security measures including password protected systems, access control protocols, limited employee access, secure cloud storage, encrypted communications where applicable, firewall and malware protection, regular backups, secure payment systems, and staff confidentiality agreements.

Despite reasonable safeguards, no system can guarantee absolute security.

13. International Data Transfers

Where business operations require the transfer of information outside South Africa, including cloud hosting services, third party providers, or international platforms, BLKSM Events will ensure adequate security safeguards, lawful processing standards, and contractual protections with third party processors.

14. Your Rights Under POPIA

As a data subject, you have the right to:

  • Access — request access to information we hold about you
  • Correction — request correction of inaccurate information
  • Deletion — request deletion where legally permissible
  • Objection — object to certain processing activities
  • Withdraw Consent — withdraw previously granted consent
  • Restrict Processing — request limitations on data processing
  • Complain — lodge complaints with the Information Regulator

15. Data Breaches

In the event of unauthorized access, loss, theft, or compromise of personal information, BLKSM Events will investigate the incident immediately, take corrective action, notify affected individuals where legally required, and notify the South African Information Regulator when required under POPIA.

16. Children's Information

We do not knowingly collect personal information from persons under the age of 18 without appropriate parental or guardian consent. Where events involve minors, consent may be required from parents or legal guardians.

17. Third Party Links

Our website, marketing materials, or event platforms may contain links to third-party websites. BLKSM Events is not responsible for privacy practices of external websites. Users should review third-party privacy policies separately.

18. Information Officer

BLKSM Events appoints an Information Officer responsible for compliance with privacy legislation, including managing privacy compliance, handling data requests, managing security protocols, responding to privacy complaints, and monitoring POPIA compliance obligations.

Privacy requests may be directed to: hello@blksm.events
